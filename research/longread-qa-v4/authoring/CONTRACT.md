User authorized a genuine reasoning redesign of Q6 after v3 remained 100. Keep all historical runs, scores, standards, and frozen banks immutable. This directory is host-only. No candidate may read it.

Produce 6 initial + 2 change questions per domain (physics, math, novel), 18 + 6 total. The unissued 36-question draft was reduced after blind review found redundant families and decorative sources. Each retained item must combine genuinely needed facts from at least two sources into dependent reasoning; source count alone is not difficulty. Initial physics weights 6,6,6,6,5,5 (34); math/novel 6,6,6,5,5,5 (33). Change physics 17,17 (34); math/novel 17,16 (33). IDs P01..P06, M01..M06, N01..N06 and XP01..XP02, XM01..XM02, XN01..XN02.

Public question schema:
`{"id":"P01","domain":"physics","question":"...","answerType":"structured","answerParts":[{"name":"first requested quantity","kind":"rational"},{"name":"second quantity","kind":"integer"},{"name":"event order","kind":"code"}],"weight":3,"scoring":"exact"}`

Answer is one string with ordered fields separated by ASCII semicolons, e.g. `"17/24;5;CADB"`. No explanations required. Each question independently specifies what each field means. `integer` accepts signed base-ten integers only; `rational` accepts an integer, finite decimal, or fraction (exact arithmetic equivalence); `code` is one nonempty uppercase alphanumeric sequence without punctuation. Codes may concatenate single-letter labels for a unique event order or selected assignment. Whitespace and code letter case do not matter. All fields must match to receive the question's weight. Do not use approximate floating point; ask exact fractions or explicitly rounded integers. Do not expose the reference answer in format examples.

Novel: at least 2 genuinely necessary chapters per question, reconstruct constraints/information available at specific times, actor knowledge, communication reach, chronology or causal counterfactuals strictly supported by text. Avoid arbitrary riddles disconnected from the novel, uncertain dates, interpretive moral judgments, and recognition via true/false statements. Where a counterfactual needs a rule, state it explicitly, then require nontrivial cross-chapter facts to apply it. A compact table of opaque event/actor codes is allowed; do not provide the factual order or outcome itself. Result must be unique.

Papers: at least 2 genuinely necessary papers per question. Avoid citing a third paper merely for a standard derivative or probability definition. Link the outputs of one paper's formula/conditions into another's domain constraints, optimize a small exact constrained decision, or reconcile nonidentical quantities/normalizations. Use more than abstract slogans. Avoid asking whether a claim is 'proven' when text obviously warns that it isn't. Do not invent scientific truth from preprints: explicitly scope to provided definitions, assumptions and reports. Original synthetic numerical inputs are allowed; they must lead to a meaningful derived result, not change source assumptions unnoticed.

No public target paper IDs, titles, authors, exact source citations or chapter numbers. No six-option answer choices, generic absolute-vs-qualified distractors, or formula/threshold supplied in the stem if retrieving and reconciling that fact is what should be tested. Candidate can use all source texts/PDFs offline. Source text in `research/longread-20261018/papers/` and `novel/` is frozen; do not change it.

Output `authoring/<domain>.json` with `{"version":"longread-qa-v4","questions":{"initial":[],"change":[]},"answers":{"initial":[],"change":[]}}`. Each host answer row: `id`, canonical `answer`, `evidence` list (same existing source/path/locator/quote format), `derivation` detailed reproducible sequence, `dependencies` array (each with source IDs and its specific indispensable contribution), `ablation` explanation of what is undetermined if one source is removed, `claimScope`, `difficultyRationale`. All quotations must be literal substrings of corpus modulo whitespace and must actually support the derivation. Use exact calculations independently (Python Fraction where relevant). Store any calculation verifier as `authoring/<domain>_calculations.py`. Do not modify common scoring code or other domains.

Independently reviewed answers must be frozen before the final candidate run. Blinded reviewers receive only public question fields and the corpus; author's answer/evidence must not be included. A question-only (no corpus) pilot is for detecting answer cues, not for predicting final model scores. Ambiguity is a defect, not a difficulty mechanism. No target final candidate score and no post-hoc answer edits.
