ONE-LEVEL DENSITY OF ZEROS OF THE RIEMANN ZETA
FUNCTION WEIGHTED BY ITS SPECIAL V ALUES
SHINGO SUGIYAMA AND ADE IRMA SURIAJAYA
Abstract.We consider the one-level density of zeros of the Riemann zeta function
weighted by its special values on the line Re(s) =σ, whereσis a fixed real number such
that 1/2⩽σ⩽1. We show that the density function for the Riemann zeta function
changes if and only ifσ= 1/2.
1.Introduction
1.1.Weighted density conjecture for a family ofL-functions.The relation be-
tween number theory and physics is an interesting subject and has been studied in several
directions. For example, the behavior ofL-functions in number theory can be studied
using random matrix theory in physics. Katz and Sarnak [13, 14] predicted the density
conjecture that low-lying zeros ofL-functions in a family should distribute like eigenvalues
of random matrices in a compact matrix group. For a familyFofL-functions, they la-
beledFby the symmetry typeG∈ {U,Sp,O,SO(even),SO(odd)}derived from compact
matrix groups. Here U denotesunitarytype, Sp denotessymplectic type, and SO(even),
SO(odd) and O refer toorthogonaltypes. More concretely, the distribution of low-lying
zeros ofL-functions inFis expected to be one of five density functionsW G of eigenvalues
of random matrices:
