1.1.On the sixth and twelfth moments.The estimateM 12(T)≪ ε T 2+ε was proved by Heath-
Brown via short second moments of zeta. The desire to improve on this bound has inspired many
developments in analytic number theory, and led to several different proofs of the same result. For
example, Iwaniec [Iwa80] deduced it from bounds for the fourth moment of zeta in short intervals,
obtained via spectral theory of automorphic forms. Jutila [Jut91] gave a different proof of Iwaniec’s
result, bypassing the appeal to spectral theory. A textbook proof is given in [Ivi03, Chapter 8].
Both Iwaniec’s and Jutila’s methods give the same bounds for a fixed GL 2 L-function in place
ofζ 2. It is worth mentioning that significantly less is known for GL 3 L-functions. The best known
bound for the second moment in thet-aspect (which should be compared toM 6(T)) isT 4/3+ε, due
to Dasgupta, Leung, and Young [DLY24]. Interestingly, the boundT 5/4+ε was recently obtained
by Aggarwal, Leung, and Munshi [ALM26] for a short second moment of lengthT 2/3.
As noted earlier, the sixth moment bound ofO ε(T 5/4+ε) follows by interpolating the fourth and
twelfth moment bounds. Expanding out with the approximate functional equation, this bound
roughly amounts to showing an asymptotic with remainderO ε(X7/6+ε) for a smoothed version of
