JESSE THORNER
Abstract.Letπandπ ′ be unitary cuspidal automorphic representations of GL(n) and
GL(n′) over a number fieldF. LetC π be the analytic conductor ofπ. We develop a new
approach to zero-free regions forL-functions via lower bounds for power sums, proving
for allε >0 the existence of ineffective constantsc=c n,F,ε >0 andc ′ =c ′
n,F,π′,ε >0
such that the standardL-functionL(s, π) satisfies
|L(σ+it, π)| ≥c(C π(|t|+ 3)) −ε, σ≥1−c(C π(|t|+ 3)) −ε
and the Rankin–SelbergL-functionL(s, π×π ′) satisfies
|L(σ+it, π×π ′)| ≥c ′(Cπ(|t|+ 3)) −ε, σ≥1−c ′(Cπ(|t|+ 3)) −ε.
Applications include improvements to the prime number theorems for theseL-functions
and new generalizations of the Brauer–Siegel theorem.
1.Introduction and main results
We develop a new method for establishing zero-free regions near Re(s) = 1 for GL(n)
standardL-functionsL(s, π) and GL(n)×GL(n ′) Rankin–SelbergL-functionsL(s, π×π ′)
over a number fieldF. In contrast with all earlier work on zero-free regions for theseL-
functions [1, 5, 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 26, 27, 30, 34, 35, 36, 37, 40, 41, 42, 44],
our method, which is inspired by proofs of log-free zero density estimates [3, 7, 39],
relies on two distinct lower bounds for power sums in complementary roles. Our results
are equivalent to certain unconditional ineffective lower bounds: IfC π is the analytic
conductor ofπ,t∈R, andε >0, then
|L(1 +it, π)| ≫ n,F,ε (Cπ(|t|+ 3)) −ε,|L(1 +it, π×π ′)| ≫ n,F,π′,ε (Cπ(|t|+ 3)) −ε.

1.3.The need for a different approach.The approach to a standard zero-free region
for anL-functionL(s) in [1, 14, 15, 17, 18, 26, 30, 44] crucially relies on constructing
an auxiliary productD(s) of shiftedL-functions such thatD(s) has nonnegative Dirich-
let coefficients and the property that if 1 +it 0 is a pole ofD(s) of orderk≥1, then
there existsℓ > ksuch that in a neighborhood of 1 +it 0,D(s) is well-approximated
by|L(1)| ℓ. This mirrors the strategy for the standard zero-free region for DirichletL-
functions. Hoffstein and Ramakrishnan [15] proved that if certain Rankin–SelbergL-
functions are modular (as conjectured by Langlands, see their Hypothesis H(π)), then one
can always construct such aD(s), resulting in the zero-free regions (1.1) and (1.5), except
forL(s, π) whenπ∈F 1 andπ=eπ. There are limited cases of self-dualπ∈ S∞
n=2 Fn, or of
Rankin–SelbergL-functionsL(s, π×π ′) not satisfying (1.4), for which existing progress
towards the modularity of Rankin–SelbergL-functions suffices to construct a suitable
D(s) [27, 34, 41, 42]. The weaker zero-free regions in [5, 13, 12, 37, 40] also rely on con-
structing a suitableD(s) with nonnegative Dirichlet coefficients, but the inequalityℓ > k
is replaced byℓ≥k. One can also use the Maaß–Selberg relations [8, 10, 16, 35], but the
resultingC π-uniformity is weaker than (1.2) or (1.6).
