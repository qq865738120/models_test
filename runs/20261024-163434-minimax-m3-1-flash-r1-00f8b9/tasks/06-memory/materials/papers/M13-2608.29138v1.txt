HAN LI AND JINGWEN HAN
Abstract. In this paper, we investigate the uniqueness and asymptotic behavior of the sta-
tionary Navier-Stokes equations in a slab domain with no-slip boundary conditions. Specif-
ically, under the given external force and the small Poiseuille flow assumptions, we prove
that there is a H 1 solution, and obtain the pointwise decay around the Poiseuille flow at
far field. Furthermore, if the force is small, the solution is shown to be unique. The key
point of the proof is the estimate of the Dirichlet integral of the solutions in the truncated
domains and the Stokes regularity estimates.
1. Introduction and Main Results
The stationary Navier-Stokes equations are of fundamental importance in fluid mechanics,
describing the steady motion of viscous incompressible fluids. In this paper, we are interested
in the uniqueness and pointwise asymptotic behavior of the solutions to the nonhomogeneous
stationary Navier-Stokes equations in a three-dimensional slab domain Ω = R2 × (0, 1), given
by:

and f = (f 1, f 2, f 3) is the external force. The slab domain is supplemented with the no-slip

(2) u = 0, at x3 = 0 and 1.
The solvability and asymptotic properties of the Stokes and Navier-Stokes equations in
domains with noncompact boundaries—such as cylindrical, quasi-cylindrical, and layer-like
outlets—have been the subject of extensive mathematical research over the past few decades.

The pioneering investigations of solutions in unbounded domains with unbounded Dirich-

Nazarov, Pileckas, and Specovius-Neugebauer [12–15,20,21], where they obtained systemati-
cally explored the solvability and asymptotic properties of weak solutions in various weighted
and anisotropic functional spaces.
In particular, Nazarov and Pileckas [13] investigated the solvability of the weak solutions
to the Navier-Stokes equations in a domain that coincides with the three-dimensional layer
R2 × (0, 1) outside a compact ball. By employing the Banach contraction mapping principle,
they constructed non-trivial solutions that asymptotically approach classical laminar flows,
such as the plane-parallel Poiseuille flow, Couette flow, and rotational flow. Specifically, for
the Poiseuille flow at far field, they proved that if the given external force and the far-field
mass flux are suﬀiciently small, there exists a unique weak solution whose perturbation field
possesses a finite Dirichlet integral. In this paper, we try to investigate the problem with
large external force.
The classical Leray problem for the stationary Navier–Stokes equations in channels and
pipes has motivated lots of works on the asymptotic behavior of solutions in domains with
noncompact boundaries. For two-dimensional channels with no-slip boundary conditions
and prescribed flux Φ , the first author and sha [10] proved existence of weak solutions for
any flux Φ , as long as the cross-section width grows sublinearly at infinity, uniqueness for
suﬀiciently small flux Φ under additional geometric assumptions. They also obtained the
pointwise decay rate of the velocity for arbitrary flux. In a complementary three-dimensional
direction, for layer-like domains that coincide outside a ball with the slab Ω = R2 × (0, 1),
Pileckas [20] established that weak solutions with nonzero flux behave at infinity like solutions
of the linear Stokes system under the certain weighted integral assumptions. This analysis
was refined by Pileckas and Specovius-Neugebauer [21], who showed that if the data decay
suﬀiciently fast, then the first three asymptotic terms of any solution growing not too fast

4. uniqueness for solutions with small force

u − W , which completes the proof of Theorem 1.1. As discussed in Section 3, while the
